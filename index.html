The line

A range in development, not a shopper listing.

We are building a line of premium protein products around novel ingredients. Precision fermentation and related biotech routes are how we source specific proteins — a supply capability, not a product name. Formats and SKUs will be confirmed with partners.

The products are food. Packs are written for Hong Kong labelling: Traditional Chinese and English. We do not print a function claim a retail compliance desk cannot stand behind.

A named protein can be expressed by a microbial host, isolated, and formulated as food. That is the method. We keep the description to what the process actually does, and we keep pack language inside what the ingredient file can support.

Partners

What we bring to a buyer or distributor.

Hong Kong is the first market. Singapore, Japan, and Korea follow as the range is ready. Mainland China opens through cross-border e-commerce as a beachhead — a channel, not a claim that domestic registration is already in hand.

For retailers

A food-positioned line with bilingual packs prepared for Hong Kong shelves, and a regional sequence so later markets do not fight the first listing. Suitable for chain grocery and drugstore buyers who already run a protein set.

For distributors

A founder-led counterpart for assortment, claims, and timing. One house to speak with on pack copy, ingredient files, and when a market actually opens — rather than a remote brand team handing over a finished imported SKU.
