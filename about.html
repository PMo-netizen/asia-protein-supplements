About

A founder-led Hong Kong company.

Asia Protein Supplements is a Hong Kong house building a protein line for Asian retail. The company is founder-led. The background is cosmetics and biotech — pack discipline from one, ingredient method from the other.

How the house is run

Small, founder-led, and written for the trade. We do not publish a founder name, a portrait, or a biography. What a buyer needs to know is on this page: Hong Kong, cosmetics and biotech, a protein line sold as food, and a short intro call.

There is no remote brand committee between a buyer and the line. The person on the call is the person who signs pack copy.

Why Hong Kong

Hong Kong is not a flag of convenience. It is the first listing, the first pack language, and the city from which the rest of the sequence is run.

A bilingual trade city

Traditional Chinese and English are how this market already labels food. The first pack is written for that, not translated later from an English master.

A listing we can prove

Hong Kong is the first shelf we will put the line on. Regional partners then have a real listing, not a slide.

Close to the region we serve

Singapore, Japan, Korea, and China cross-border are the next conversations, from here — not from a Western headquarters writing Asia as a later chapter.

What this is

We are not a shopper brand yet, and we do not pretend to be. We are a small house asking for a short conversation with people who already buy or distribute food in this region.
