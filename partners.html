Partners

How we work with buyers and distributors.

We speak with three kinds of counterpart: retail chains, regional distributors, and China cross-border platforms. This is a commercial sequence, not a regulatory matrix.

Who we speak with

Retail chains

A food-positioned line with bilingual packs prepared for Hong Kong shelves, and a regional sequence so later markets do not fight the first listing. Written for chain grocery and drugstore buyers who already run a protein set — not for a blank-slate brand launch.

Regional distributors

A founder-led counterpart for assortment, pack copy, ingredient files, and when a market actually opens. One house to speak with, rather than a remote brand team handing over a finished imported SKU.

China cross-border platforms

Cross-border e-commerce first, so the line can sit on Tmall Global, JD Worldwide, and related platforms while general trade remains a later discussion. The beachhead is a channel choice, not a domestic-registration claim.

What a first conversation covers

A short call. We are not pitching a finished shopper brand. We are asking whether there is a fit on market, channel, and timing.

  1. Market and channel

    Which market you actually cover, and whether that is chain retail, wholesale, or a platform.

  2. Where it would sit

    Where the line would sit in an existing protein set — not a blank-slate brand story.

  3. Pack and file

    Pack language, ingredient files, and what a compliance desk will need to see.

  4. What ready means

    Listing window, territory, and who signs. We would rather hear a real constraint than a polite maybe.

What we need from a partner

We are a small house. The first conversations are more useful when the other side is equally specific.

  1. A named owner

    A buyer or category owner, not a general mailbox.

  2. A territory that is yours

    A channel and geography you already cover.

  3. A hand in assortment

    A willingness to work on the range while it is still being set — formats, not only a purchase order.

  4. Hong Kong in the lead

    Patience for Hong Kong to lead. We will not ask you to list a market we have not prepared.

Where we start

Hong Kong first; Singapore, Japan, and Korea as the range is ready; China through cross-border e-commerce as a beachhead. Order of work, not a calendar in months.
