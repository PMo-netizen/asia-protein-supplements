The line

A range in development, not a shopper listing.

We are building a line of premium protein products around novel ingredients. Formats and SKUs will be confirmed with partners. That is why this is a line — not a named consumer product waiting on a listing sheet.

Precision fermentation and related biotech routes are how we source specific proteins. That is a supply capability, not a product name, and not a claim we print on pack.

The products are food. Packs are written for Hong Kong labelling: Traditional Chinese and English. We do not print a function claim a retail compliance desk cannot stand behind.

We keep the description to what the process actually does, and we keep pack language inside what the ingredient file can support. Partners see the file. Shoppers see food.

Why a line, not a single SKU

A single imported item arrives finished. A line is still being set — assortment, pack, and timing — with the people who will actually list it.

Assortment, not one SKU

Partners help set formats and how the range sits in an existing protein set. You are not receiving a finished imported item with the campaign already written.

One house, several expressions

The same sourcing discipline runs through the range, so a later item does not fight the first listing on claim, pack, or origin story.

Timing with the trade

What goes on shelf is confirmed with the buyer. We do not ask a market to list what the range has not yet prepared.

Pack and position

Sold as food

The category is food. That decision governs pack copy, the ingredient file, and what a compliance desk will be asked to accept. It is not a later rewrite.

Traditional Chinese and English

The first pack is written for Hong Kong labelling, in both languages. Later markets get pack written for those trades — not a last-mile translation of an English master.

What we will not print

No function claim a retail compliance desk cannot stand behind. Novel ingredients stay in the sourcing story and the file; they do not become a headline on pack.

The method, in one paragraph

A named protein can be expressed by a microbial host, isolated, and formulated as food. That is the method. We do not dress it as a consumer story, and we do not ask a buyer to defend language the file cannot support.
